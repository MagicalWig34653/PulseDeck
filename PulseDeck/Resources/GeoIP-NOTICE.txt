IP geolocation data

PulseDeck looks up countries and network operators for remote addresses offline, using a database
bundled with the app (GeoIP.bin). No address leaves your Mac for this. The data was built on 2026-10-09 from:

- IPtoASN (https://iptoasn.com), republished by the ip-location-db project
  (https://github.com/sapics/ip-location-db), made available under the Public Domain Dedication and
  License (PDDL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/pddl/1-0/
- The CloudFront address ranges published by Amazon Web Services
  (https://ip-ranges.amazonaws.com/ip-ranges.json).

The country shown is the registration country of the network operator, not the physical location of a
server. Providers are identified by autonomous system number for a curated list of well-known networks;
other addresses show only a country.
